# Okutama fieldwork media

These files are web derivatives of photographs in the team fieldwork folder used for the Issue #270 demo convergence. They are evidence for the pictured scene only; they do not verify operating hours, availability, access conditions, safety, or any other business claim.

## Rights and handling boundary

- Source folder: [Tokyo Mogu Mogu fieldwork photos](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1p4seRQO1FgJ_KIym38skBnHLcsQUfp8a)
- Access and mapping were reviewed on 2026-08-23.
- Issues #258 and #270 authorize using selected fieldwork photos in the Tokyo Mogu Mogu project demo. The Drive folder does not publish creator, copyright-owner, model-release, or reusable-license metadata, so this repository does **not** claim a public license or permission for reuse outside that project scope.
- Only frames without identifiable people were selected.
- Bundled files are resized WebP derivatives. The multi-megabyte originals are not shipped.
- Derivatives were auto-oriented, resized to 640, 960, and 1440 CSS-pixel source widths, encoded as WebP, and stripped of EXIF, device, timestamp, and GPS metadata.

## Deliberate exclusions

Other folder photos were not copied into this set. Photos containing identifiable people were excluded, ambiguous scenery was not attached to a named venue, and the gelato photo was excluded because its prominent price signage could become a stale business claim.
